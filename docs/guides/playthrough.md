# Shabab 2 Playthrough: start to finish, every mod on the way

This is one route through the whole pack, in order, from your first tree to the Ender Dragon. It is
written so you touch every big mod at the point where it makes sense, instead of finding out three
weeks in that a mod existed.

The other guides go deeper on single topics and this one links to them:

- [Systems Guide](systems.md): keys, Epic Fight, Solo Leveling, MineColonies, vehicles, voice chat
- [Content Guide](content.md): every dimension and how to open it, every boss and how to start it
- [Progression Route](progression.md): boss order and the best armour
- [Boss Rewards](boss-rewards.md): HP and loot for every boss
- [All keybinds](../keybinds-shabab2.md)

Recipes change between mod versions and some are tweaked for this server, so this guide names items
and tells you where they matter. For the exact recipe, use JEI (see below). JEI is always right.

## Part 0: Before you take a step

### Look things up in game

| Tool | How |
|---|---|
| **JEI** (item list on the right of your inventory) | Hover an item: `R` shows how to make it, `U` shows what it is used in. Type a mod's name with `@` in front (`@twilightforest`) to list everything from that mod. |
| **Just Enough Resources** | In JEI, ore heights and mob drops. Look an ore up before you dig for it. |
| **Just Enough Breeding / Professions** | In JEI, what each animal eats and what each villager job block is. |
| **Jade** | Look at any block or mob for its name, mod, health and progress. |
| **Enchantment Descriptions** | Hover an enchanted book and it says what the enchantment does. |
| **FTB Quests** | The book icon at the top left of your inventory. Chapters for the Overworld, Nether, End, Aether, Twilight Forest, Blue Skies, Deeper and Darker, dragons, storage, villagers and decoration. Some quests hand out rewards. |
| **Patchouli books** | Several mods ship an in-game manual. If you are given one or find one, read it. |

### The rules of this server that change how you play

- **You take half damage from mobs, but bosses have huge health pools.** Fights are long, not
  unfair. Fall damage is not halved, so Feather Falling matters more than usual.
- **Dying is not the end of your items.** You leave a **corpse** (Corpse mod) holding everything.
  `/back` takes you to your death spot once a minute. After 1 hour the corpse rots and anyone can
  loot it, so go back for it.
- **Bosses are how you level.** Every boss pays Solo Leveling XP to your whole nearby party, and
  drops a bounty of diamonds, emeralds and more. First kill of each boss type pays double. A
  fight where nobody in the party dies pays 50% more.
- **Normal hostile mobs pay a little Solo Leveling XP too** (about 10 zombies for your first level).
- **Gates** (Solo Leveling dungeons) open about every 2 hours of server time. Chat announces each
  one with its coordinates. You walk there; there is no teleport button.
- **Night is skipped when 30% of online players sleep.**

### Team up

- Party up with your friends (`P`, FTB Teams). Party members share boss XP and Gate XP.
- Claim your base with **Open Parties and Claims** (`O`): open the menu, claim the chunks around
  your base, and add your party. Claimed chunks are protected from other players and from explosions.
- **Simple Voice Chat**: `\` opens the voice menu, `-` makes a group. Proximity voice is on by default.
- **Ping Wheel**: `Right Shift` drops a marker that your party sees.

## Part 1: Day one (the first hour)

### Wood, tools, a place to sleep

1. **Punch a tree.** **FallingTree** is installed: break the bottom log with an axe and the whole
   tree comes down.
2. Make a crafting table and wooden tools, then stone tools. **Visual Workbench** keeps the items
   visible on the table, and **Mouse Tweaks** lets you drag and scroll stacks.
3. **Carry On**: hold `Left Shift` and right-click a chest or a small animal with an empty hand to
   pick it up and carry it. Moving a full chest to a new base is one trip.
4. Craft a **sleeping bag** (Comforts). It lets you skip the night without moving your spawn point,
   so you can sleep anywhere while exploring. A **hammock** skips the day the same way.
5. Use **Mouse Middle** in any inventory to sort it (Client Sort).

### First food

- Kill animals for meat, then get **Farmer's Delight** started as early as you can. Its food is
  far better than vanilla's and gives long buffs:
  - **Cutting Board** (cut ingredients with a knife or tool)
  - **Cooking Pot** (needs heat under it: a campfire, fire, or a Stove)
  - **Skillet** and **Stove**
  - **Flint Knife** first, then iron. Knives also get extra drops from animals.
- The add-ons (Ocean's, Crabber's, My Nether's, Ender's, Twilight's Flavors, Delightful, Autochef's)
  add more ingredients as you reach new places. You will meet them along the way.
- **Rice, tomatoes, cabbages and onions** grow in wild patches. Grab seeds of each the first time you
  see them.

### A backpack

Craft the first **Inmis backpack** (Frayed). `H` opens it while it is in your inventory. Each tier
(Frayed, Plated, Gilded, Bejeweled, Blazing, Withered, Endless) is crafted from the one before,
so keep upgrading it as materials come in. Check JEI under `@inmis`.

### Find a village

Villages are rebuilt by **ChoiceTheorem's Overhauled Village**, **Towns and Towers**, **Villages &
Pillages** and **Moog's Missing Villages**, so they are bigger and every biome has its own style.

- **Guard Villagers** protect them. Give a guard better gear and they get stronger.
- **Villager Names**, **TownTalk** and **Villagers Plus** (new jobs) make them feel alive.
- **Bountiful**: a **Bounty Board** in the village posts tasks for rewards. Easy early emeralds.
- **Trading Post**: place one near villagers and you see every nearby trade in one screen.
- **Chef's Delight** villagers trade Farmer's Delight food and tools.

## Part 2: Set up a real base

Pick a spot, claim it (`O`), and build these as the materials arrive:

| What | Mod | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Magnum Torch** | Magnum Torch | Stops mob spawns in a big radius. The Diamond torch stops hostile mobs. Put one in the middle of the base. |
| **Waystone** | Waystones | Teleport between waystones you have visited. Natural ones spawn in villages, craft your own for home. |
| **Storage network** | Simple Storage Network | A Storage Network Master, Storage Nodes on your chests, and a Request Table: every chest in one searchable screen. |
| **Elevator** | OpenBlocks Elevator | Stack two elevator blocks in a column; jump to go up, sneak to go down. |
| **Farm** | Smarter Farmers, Incubation, Serene Seasons | Farmer villagers replant. Chickens use nests. Seasons change, and crops only grow in their seasons (check the crop tooltip). Craft a **Calendar** to see the season. |
| **Kitchen** | Farmer's Delight | Cooking Pot, Stove, Cutting Board, a few Baskets. |
| **Enchanting room** | Easy Magic | The table keeps your lapis and item inside, and you can re-roll the offered enchantments. |

### Make it look good

This pack is full of building mods. Use them; nobody else will.

- **Chipped**: a set of workbenches that turn any block into dozens of variants.
- **Handcrafted**, **Another Furniture**: chairs, tables, beds, shelves, counters.
- **Supplementaries** and **Amendments**: jars, sacks, signposts, flags, globes, item shelves, ropes.
- **Twigs**, **Decorative Blocks**, **Stoneworks**, **Diagonal Fences/Walls**, **Vertical Slabs**.
- **Quark**: a huge set of blocks and small features. Every wood type works with every mod thanks to
  **Every Compat**.
- **Toni's Immersive Lanterns**, **Hearths**, **Gallery** (paintings), **Multi-Piston**.

## Part 3: Learn to fight (before you go anywhere dangerous)

### Epic Fight

Combat in this pack is **Epic Fight**: combos, dodges, guards, and a special move per weapon.

- `K` switches between **Battle** and **Mining** mode. Combos only work in Battle mode.
- Left-click combos, hold right-click to guard, `Mouse 5` to dodge, `N` to lock on.
- `=` fires your weapon's innate skill.
- **Skill Books** drop from any mob you kill yourself (about 2.5%). Right-click to learn, then press
  `` ` `` to open the Skill Editor and slot it. Passives work always; weapon skills need the right
  weapon type.
- **Weapons of Miracles** and **EpicFight: Resurrection** add more weapons and skills to the same system.
- **Shield Expansion** makes shields matter: time your block for a parry.

Spend twenty minutes fighting zombies in Battle mode before your first boss. It is a different game.

### Solo Leveling: awaken

This is the headline system and you level it the whole time you play.

- `;` opens the **System panel**: level, stats, skills, job.
- Every level gives stat points. **Strength** multiplies all your damage. **Vitality** keeps you
  alive. **Intelligence** powers shadow soldiers later.
- `'` shows your quest info. Do the **Daily Quest**. Skipping it sends you to the **Punishment
  Zone**, and you come back exactly where you left.
- `Z X C V` are job abilities, `R` uses the selected skill, `B` cycles skills.

### Your first Gates

When chat says a **Gate** has opened, take the coordinates and go. Ranks run **E, D, C, B, A, S**.
Start with E and D.

- Clear the boss inside before the timer ends. Fail and the monsters break out into the Overworld.
- You come back to the exact spot you entered from.
- Kills inside Gates count for the **Gate kills leaderboard**.

## Part 4: Iron age and the Overworld

### Mining

- **Vein Mining**: hold `Caps Lock` while breaking an ore to mine the whole vein.
- **YUNG's Better Mineshafts** and **YUNG's Cave Biomes** make caves worth exploring.
- **Galosphere** adds crystal caves with **Silver** (Sterling armour) and new mobs.
- **Goblin Traders** live underground and trade things no villager will.
- Do not mine for diamonds for long. **Bosses drop them** (see below).

### Explore

- **Xaero's Minimap** (top right) and **Xaero's World Map** (`M`). Set waypoints for everything.
- **Nature's Compass**: craft it and pick any biome; it points there.
- Biomes come from **Biomes O' Plenty**, **Geophilic**, **Climate Rivers** and **Stony Cliffs**.
- Structures to look for: **When Dungeons Arise** (huge landmark dungeons), **YUNG's** better
  dungeons, strongholds, witch huts, jungle temples and ocean monuments, **Dungeons and Taverns**,
  **Stalwart Dungeons**, **Repurposed Structures**, **Structory**, **Explorations**, **AdoraBuild**,
  **Philip's Ruins**, **Formations**, **Moog's Voyager Structures**, **Farmers Structures**.
- **Alex's Mobs** is everywhere: about 80 animals, most with a use. Craft the **Animal Dictionary**
  to read about them.
- **Friends & Foes**: Copper Golem, Moobloom, Glare, Rascal, Iceologer.

### First minibosses (iron and diamond gear)

These are the first real fights and they pay in diamonds: about 5 diamonds per 1,000 HP.

| Boss | HP | Where |
|---|---:|---|
| Mutant Zombie / Skeleton / Creeper | 800 to 1,200 | Overworld at night |
| The Conjurer | 900 | Theatre in a Dark Forest. Fight through the illagers first. |
| Ferrous Wroughtnaut | 1,200 | Wrought Chamber, underground |
| Frostmaw | 1,500 | Snowy mountains (the big pile of fur) |
| Lord Pumpkinhead | 2,000 | Born in Chaos structure |

Also watch for **Born in Chaos** horror mobs, **Illager Invasion** and **Raided** raids, and
**It Takes a Pillage** outposts. Raids are much worse than vanilla.

### Gear up

- Full **iron**, then **diamond**, then **netherite**.
- **Galosphere Sterling** armour is a good side option.
- Put a **Charm of Undying** or **Void Totem** in your Curios slot when you get one: it is an extra
  life that also saves you from falling into the void.

### Enchanting

| Mod | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Easy Magic** | Enchanting table keeps items, re-roll the offers. |
| **Easy Anvils** | Anvils keep their items and are easier to use. |
| **Easy Disenchanting** | Pull enchantments off an item onto a book. |
| **XP Tome** | Store your XP in a book so you do not lose it. |

The trick: bosses drop gear enchanted past the vanilla limit (Protection VII, Power VIII). Disenchant
the pieces you do not want and put those books on the set you wear. Full list of what to put on each
slot is in [Progression Route](progression.md#enchantments--the-part-that-actually-matters).

## Part 5: Level 40, your job

At **level 40** the System gives you a **Job Change Quest Key**. Clear the quest and you get one of:

- **Shadow Monarch**: raise dead mobs as shadow soldiers. Igris, Tusk and Beru get half your health
  and armour, others a quarter. Their damage scales with your Intelligence.
- **Grand Mage**: magic damage.
- **Frost Monarch**: ice.

Do the quest again to re-roll; you never get the same job twice in a row. You get another key every
50 levels from level 100.

A **Reborn Elixir** (Class Selector ringed with 8 ancient debris) refunds every stat point if you
want to rebuild. `/invest 50` makes each `+` in the stats panel spend 50 points at once.

## Part 6: The Nether

Same portal as vanilla, very different place.

- **BetterNether**, **Bygone Nether**, **Soulful Nether**, **Jaden's Nether Expansion** and
  **Formations Nether** rebuild the biomes. **YUNG's Better Nether Fortresses** and **Moog's Nether
  Structures** rebuild the buildings.
- New gear and ores from BetterNether: **Cincinnasite** and **Nether Ruby**.
- **My Nether's Delight** adds Nether cooking.
- **Bartering Station** trades gold with piglins automatically.
- **Nether Chested**: put chests on striders.
- Get **ancient debris** and go **netherite**. **Advanced Netherite** then upgrades netherite gear
  with iron, gold, emerald and diamond tiers. Look up `@advancednetherite` in JEI.

The **Wither** (4,000 HP) and the **Warden** (6,000 HP) are your next real fights. The Warden
matters: it drops the **Heart of the Deep**, which opens the Otherside (Part 9).

Careful: the Nether also has Cataclysm's **Netherite Monstrosity** (12,000 HP). Leave it until
Part 10.

## Part 7: The Twilight Forest (the real start)

The best first dimension. Cheap portal, gentle boss ladder, great gear.

**Portal:** dig a 2x2 pool of water, put flowers or mushrooms on the grass around it, throw a
diamond in.

Kill the bosses in order. Each one unlocks the next area; skipping ahead gets you blocked.

| # | Boss | HP | Unlocks / drops |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Naga | 2,000 | Naga Scale armour |
| 2 | Lich | 3,500 | Lich scepters |
| 3 | Minoshroom | 5,500 | Bosses from here also drop ancient debris and netherite scrap |
| 4 | Hydra | 8,000 | Fiery Blood: **Fiery** armour and tools. Echo shards start dropping. |
| 5 | Knight Phantom | 11,000 | **Phantom** armour. Enchanted golden apples start dropping. |
| 6 | Ur-Ghast | 15,000 | Dark Tower |
| 7 | Alpha Yeti | 20,000 | **Yeti** armour |
| 8 | Snow Queen | 26,000 | Aurora Palace, end of the Forest |

Things to make here:

- **Ironwood** and **Steeleaf** gear early, **Knightmetal** from the Knight Stronghold, **Fiery**
  from the Hydra. Fiery and Phantom carry you out of this dimension.
- **Uncrafting Table**: takes crafted items back apart. Great for salvaging boss gear.
- **Magic Map** and **Maze Map** to find structures and solve the Labyrinth.
- **Twilight's Flavors & Delight** adds Twilight cooking.
- Boss **trophies**. **Umbral Skies** adds trophy and glove items that link the Forest and the Aether.

By the end of the Forest you should be several hundred levels into Solo Leveling.

## Part 8: The middle dimensions (any order, do all of them)

### The Aether (the best armour route)

**Portal:** a Glowstone frame, pour water into it.

Expanded by **Aether: Redux**, **Deep Aether**, **Lost Content**, **Aether Villages** and several
small add-ons. It is a full progression on its own:

1. **Skyroot** tools first (double drops from Aether blocks), then **Holystone**, **Zanite**, and
   **Gravitite** (high jump).
2. Collect **Ambrosium** for torches and healing.
3. Dungeons in order, each needs the key from the last:

| Boss | HP | Drops |
|---|---:|---|
| Slider (Bronze Dungeon) | 22,000 | Bronze key loot |
| Valkyrie Queen (Silver Dungeon) | 32,000 | **Valkyrie** armour |
| Sun Spirit (Gold Dungeon) | 42,000 | **Phoenix** armour (fire immune) |

4. **Deep Aether**: **Skyjade** and **Stratus** gear, top tier. Its boss is the **Eye of the
   Storm** (45,000 HP).
5. **Lost Content**: the **Aerwhale King** (52,000 HP).
6. **Treasure Reforging** lets you reforge dungeon loot. **Protect Your Moa** keeps your Moa safe.

Ride a **Moa** or a **Flying Pig**. Do not fall off. Feather Falling.

### Blue Skies (two dimensions)

**Portal:** find a **Gatekeeper**'s house in plains, mountains or snow. Buy his **Zeal Lighter**
with emeralds and light his frame. One frame leads to the **Everbright**, another to the
**Everdawn**.

- Make the local tools and armour: **Pyrope**, **Aquite**, **Diopside**, **Charoite**,
  **Horizonite**. Tools from outside the dimension work badly there.
- Bosses: Summoner 20,000, Starlit Crusher 24,000, Alchemist 28,000, Arachnarch 32,000.

### The Undergarden

**Portal:** a **Catalyst** (4 iron, 4 gold, 1 diamond). Build a Stone Brick or Deepslate Brick
frame in the Overworld and right-click the inside with it.

- Gear: **Cloggrum**, then **Froststeel** and **Utherium**.
- Boss: **Forgotten Guardian**, 16,000 HP.
- Poisonous. Bring food and milk.

## Part 9: The deep places

### The Deep Dark and the Otherside

- **Deeper and Darker** and **DnT Ancient City Overhaul** rebuild the Ancient Cities around Y -52.
- Kill the **Warden** for the **Heart of the Deep**. Clear the sculk off the big archway in the
  middle of an Ancient City and right-click the frame with the Heart. That is the portal to the
  **Otherside**.
- Write down your portal coordinates. The Otherside is huge and you cannot easily find your way out.
- Gear: **Resonarium** and Warden-themed items. Swift Sneak on your leggings.

### The Lost Cities

Not a portal but a ritual: put a **bed on top of two Diamond Blocks**, surround it with **6 skulls**,
and sleep in it. A whole world of ruined skyscrapers full of loot and mobs.

## Part 10: Cataclysm (the difficulty spike)

**L_Ender's Cataclysm** bosses live in big arenas across the Overworld, Nether and End.

| # | Boss | HP | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Netherite Monstrosity | 12,000 | Nether |
| 2 | Ender Guardian | 16,000 | Walk to the altar; the floor drops you into the arena |
| 3 | **Ignis** | 22,000 | Kill the Ignited Revenants for **Burning Ashes**, use them on the Altar of Fire. Drops **Ignitium**: the best armour in the pack, and an **Ignitium Elytra Chestplate** |
| 3 | Maledictus | 22,000 | Right-click its grave |
| 4 | The Harbinger, Scylla | 32,000 | Their own structures |
| 5 | The Leviathan | 48,000 | Sunken City: Abyssal Sacrifice into the Altar of the Abyss |
| 6 | Ancient Remnant | 75,000 | The hardest non-dragon fight |

Cataclysm armour has abilities on keys: `[` ability, `]` helmet, `,` chestplate, `.` boots.

Bosses over 50,000 HP pay raw **Skill Points** and are announced to the whole server with fireworks.

Around the same power level, try:

- **Bosses'Rise**: souls-like bosses (Infernal Dragon, Kraken and others), each one's drop unlocks
  the next. Bind the **Dodge Roll** key first (Options, Controls).
- **Mowzie's Mobs**: Barako the Sun Chief.
- **Born in Chaos**: Sir Pumpkinhead, Fallen Chaos Knight, Nightmare Stalker.

## Part 11: Side projects (start any time)

These do not fit in a fight order. Pick them up whenever you have a quiet evening.

### MineColonies

Build a town with NPC workers. Craft a **Supply Camp**, place it, get the **Town Hall** and
**Builder's Hut**, then hire citizens and place huts: farmer, miner, guard, cook and more. They
build from your materials. Good co-op project: one player keeps the builders fed.
[MineColonies schematics guide](minecolonies-schematics.md).

### Immersive Vehicles

Cars, trucks, planes and helicopters from the **Official Content Pack**. Craft the vehicle benches,
build parts, assemble, fuel up. A plane is the fastest way to cross the map before you have an
elytra.

### Dragons

**Dragon Mounts: Legacy**: hatch a dragon egg and raise it into a mount. The breed depends on where
the egg hatches, so try different places. The Ender Dragon's egg is the first you will get.

### Pets and animals

- **RevampedWolf**: better wolves.
- **Pet Cemetery**: bring a dead pet back.
- **Incubation**: chicken nests and egg handling.

## Part 12: The End and the Ender Dragon (the final wall)

The **Ender Dragon has 150,000 HP** and pays **750,000 XP**. It is the last fight, made for the whole
server at once. It fights in four phases:

1. **75%**: Shadow phantoms dive on everyone.
2. **50%**: enrage. More phantoms and a **Void barrage** of fire from above. Keep moving.
3. **25%**: second wind. It heals once and summons the **Herald of the Void**, a netherite mini-boss.
   Kill the Herald first; it always drops its sword.
4. Finish the dragon.

Bring Ignitium or the best set you have, Protection VII from boss drops, Feather Falling, a Charm of
Undying, Farmer's Delight meals, and a party.

### After the dragon

- **BetterEnd** turns the outer End islands into full biomes with **Thallasium**, **Terminite** and
  **Aeternium** gear. **YUNG's Better End Island** rebuilds the main island.
- **Moog's End Structures** and the reworked **End Cities**.
- **Elytra**: wear it in the **Elytra Slot** so you keep your chestplate.
- **Ender's Delight**: End cooking.
- **Enderdragon Loot**: the dragon drops much more than vanilla.

## Your stats at every stage

| Stage | Focus |
|---|---|
| Levels 1 to 40 | Strength and Vitality, about even |
| After your job | Strength until bosses melt; Intelligence first if you are Shadow Monarch |
| Before 20,000 HP bosses | Vitality, so one hit does not end the fight |
| Endgame | Respec with a Reborn Elixir into whatever you play most |
